<h1 align="center">🥶 SUBZERO</h1>

<h3 align="center"> A normal way to get normals, entirely on your machine.</h3>
Built as a free alternative to [SwitchLight Studio](https://www.beeble.ai/) after it turned into a subscription based model. Runs entirely on your machine, no need to pay $504 a year.<br>
